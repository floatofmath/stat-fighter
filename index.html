About Stat Fighter ’89

Plan sample sizes and power with arcade fighters: t-tests (pooled or Welch), ANOVA and ANCOVA, correlation and regression, rank tests, proportions, chi-square and non-inferiority for proportions, log-rank event counts with an optional enrolment step, vaccine efficacy from case counts, negative binomial event rates, Bonferroni families, non-inferiority and equivalence (TOST, including 2×2 crossover), and group sequential designs with interim looks. Calculations run on your device.

The Training Room converts reported values (CV, CIs, medians, odds ratios…) into what each fighter asks for. After a fight, the arena drafts a protocol paragraph for the sample size section. The Matchmaker suggests a fighter from three questions about your study.

Your hi-score table and drafts are kept in this browser only. Nothing leaves the device.

Install for offline play

On iPhone or iPad, open in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen menu.

Once the footer says Offline ready, you can calculate without a connection.

Calculation limits

Results assume complete data and a maximum of 1,000,000 participants, pairs, or observations. Two-sided t-tests use both rejection tails.
