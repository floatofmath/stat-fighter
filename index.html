About Stat Fighter ’89

Plan sample sizes and power for fifteen methods: t-tests, ANOVA, correlation and regression, rank tests, proportions and chi-square, log-rank event counts, Bonferroni families, and non-inferiority and equivalence (TOST) designs. Calculations run on your device.

Stat Fighter ’89 is a planning aid. Results depend entirely on the assumptions you enter and on each method’s stated model. It comes with no warranty and is not a substitute for review by a qualified statistician, particularly for clinical, regulatory or bioequivalence studies.

Install for offline play

On iPhone or iPad, open in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use your browser’s Install app or Add to Home screen menu.

Once the footer says Offline ready, you can calculate without a connection.

Calculation limits

Results assume complete data and a maximum of 1,000,000 participants, pairs, or observations. Two-sided t-tests use both rejection tails.
